# CreatorPilot Design References

**Research date:** 2026-07-18  
**Scope:** Creator dashboard, multi-agent workflow, script editing, review, and
storyboard production  
**Method:** Official product pages and official help documentation were reviewed.
Patterns are adapted to CreatorPilot; brand assets and complete layouts are not
copied.

## 1. YouTube Studio

- **Pages:** Creator dashboard and content management
- **URLs:** <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7548152> and
  <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2673341>
- **Why selected:** It is the most familiar professional workspace for the target
  user and demonstrates how recent content, status, and next actions coexist.
- **Adapted pattern:** Persistent left navigation, compact recent-project rows,
  thumbnail-led identification, and an overview that leads into a specific asset.
- **CreatorPilot adaptation:** Navigation is limited to Dashboard and Projects;
  project rows prioritize production stage instead of channel analytics.
- **Must not copy:** YouTube branding, iconography, analytics cards, red accent,
  navigation labels, or monetization language.

## 2. Descript

- **Pages:** Editor interface, Edit like a doc, and Scenes overview
- **URLs:** <https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/37585546799757-The-editor-interface>,
  <https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/15726742913933-Edit-like-a-doc>, and
  <https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10248939749517-Scenes-overview>
- **Why selected:** CreatorPilot also treats narration as editable production
  material and connects script sections with visual scenes.
- **Adapted pattern:** Document-like script editor, persistent metadata, explicit
  scene boundaries, and a restrained editing workspace.
- **CreatorPilot adaptation:** Script sections remain semantic and editable while
  AI agent status stays outside the document surface.
- **Must not copy:** Descript layout, Underlord framing, icons, timeline behavior,
  editor chrome, or proprietary terminology.

## 3. Frame.io

- **Page:** Capture, Review, Deliver and review/approval guidance
- **URLs:** <https://frame.io/capture-review-deliver> and
  <https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/workfront/using/review-and-approve-work/document-approvals-overview>
- **Why selected:** It models professional review as an explicit production state,
  not a conversational exchange.
- **Adapted pattern:** Clear approval status, side-by-side evidence and feedback,
  revision instructions tied to a specific asset version.
- **CreatorPilot adaptation:** Potential phrase overlap is shown as paired text
  evidence with a direct revise-or-approve decision.
- **Must not copy:** Frame.io player, annotation tools, comment UI, purple branding,
  or collaboration model.

## 4. Runway

- **Pages:** AI Video Generator and AI Video Editor
- **URLs:** <https://runwayml.com/product/ai-video-generator> and
  <https://runwayml.com/product/ai-video-editor>
- **Why selected:** It separates starting inputs, generation, refinement, and
  export—important states for an asynchronous AI production flow.
- **Adapted pattern:** Visible generation lifecycle, version-safe regeneration,
  preview before commitment, and a strong final output boundary.
- **CreatorPilot adaptation:** Rendering exposes named production tasks and a
  percent complete value rather than an indefinite spinner.
- **Must not copy:** Runway's dark visual identity, gradients, model selector,
  generation grid, prompts, or media.

## 5. Adobe Express Video

- **Page:** Create videos and scene view
- **URL:** <https://helpx.adobe.com/uk/express/web/video-creation-and-editing/create-videos/video.html>
- **Why selected:** Its scene view makes a video understandable as ordered,
  editable segments without requiring a full professional NLE timeline.
- **Adapted pattern:** Scene sequence, duration per scene, thumbnail, caption, and
  bulk production settings near the preview.
- **CreatorPilot adaptation:** A structured storyboard list displays narration,
  visual query, transition, caption, and duration for each generated scene.
- **Must not copy:** Adobe branding, toolbar, templates, timeline, stock assets,
  effects, or export controls.

## 6. Subscribr

- **Page:** AI YouTube Script Generator and product workflow
- **URL:** <https://subscribr.ai/>
- **Reviewed:** 2026-07-22
- **Why selected:** Its creator workflow separates research and writing into
  focused stages, keeping the current decision easier to scan.
- **Adapted pattern:** Progressive disclosure, a short summary first, and a small
  number of actionable story decisions before drafting.
- **CreatorPilot adaptation:** Reference analysis shows one story sentence, a
  five-step flow, three decisions (Opening, Build, Payoff), and two or three
  reusable instructions. Evidence and transcript details remain collapsed.
- **Must not copy:** Subscribr branding, orange styling, navigation labels,
  chat/editor layout, proprietary prompts, or product language.

## Synthesis

CreatorPilot uses YouTube Studio's creator-oriented orientation, Descript's
script-first editing, Frame.io's explicit review decision, Runway's asynchronous
generation lifecycle, Adobe Express's approachable scene model, and Subscribr's
focused progressive disclosure. The result uses an editorial neutral palette,
dense but readable work surfaces, lines and alignment before cards,
text-plus-shape status indicators, and no chatbot or generic AI gradient
treatment.
